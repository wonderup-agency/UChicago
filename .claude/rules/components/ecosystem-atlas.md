# ecosystem-atlas

**File**: `src/components/ecosystem-atlas.js` + `src/styles/ecosystem-atlas.css`
**Webflow attribute**: `data-component="ecosystem-atlas"` (inside the "Section / Atlas Map" component, page `/ecosystem-atlas`)
**Data**: `src/data/atlas.json` (real lab data, built by `scripts/atlas-build.js` from `prototype/ecosystem-platform/index.html`) and `src/data/atlas-world.json` (world-atlas 110m TopoJSON, Natural Earth, public domain)
**Libraries**: `d3-geo` (projections, paths), `topojson-client` (TopoJSON → GeoJSON)

## What it does

- Draws the map in `[data-atlas-stage]` as SVG with d3-geo (one projection that can be NaturalEarth1 flat or Orthographic globe). Land = one merged silhouette + per-country fills (no stroke) + a borders mesh; the graticule is hidden (Figma shows a clean globe). Default globe view `GLOBE_HOME = [55, -38]`. Drag rotates the globe.
- Globe sizing (`globeFit`): the whole globe always fits the stage. It sits on the stage bottom (16px gap) and grows, by binary search, as large as it can without touching `.atlas_intro` / `.atlas_controls` when they overlap the stage (measured in `resize()`), capped at half the stage width. The filters panel floats over the lower part of the globe via its negative top margin in Webflow (`atlas_panel` -18rem desktop / -12rem tablet, `atlas_stage` 56rem / 42rem).
- Collapsed panel keeps a bottom margin (`keepSpace`: overlap minus button height) so the page below stays under the map; recomputed on resize (`syncPanelSpace`).
- Load animation (`playIntro`, once, when the stage is 20% in view via IntersectionObserver): map layer fades and scales 0.9→1 (1.2s ease-out), beacons pop in by rank (45ms stagger, slight overshoot), labels fade in last. Skipped with reduced motion.
- Flat ↔ globe is one continuous projection morph (d3 `geoProjectionMutator` blending NaturalEarth1Raw and OrthographicRaw, as in d3's "Orthographic to Equirectangular" example): alpha, rotation, scale and translate share one eased clock (1.3s, cubic in-out) and the globe lands straight on its centered view, no extra spin. `foldAngle(alpha)` clips the far side just before the blended projection folds over itself (where horizontal scale drops to `0.5·√(1-alpha)` of the centre value), reaching exactly 90° at alpha 1; null below alpha ≈ 0.26 (no fold). `morphClip` composes that clip circle with the antimeridian cut, because a clip circle wider than a hemisphere alone lets antimeridian-crossing shapes wrap across the map as straight lines. Beacon visibility follows the same angle, and the sphere fill/outline fade in from alpha 0.25 to 0.85.
- Data model (`table()`): each entity's value is its latest published year (years differ per entity), with its n. Rows are filtered by Level (`city` entities vs countries; country-only measures, `cityLevel: false`, force Level to country: the dropdown stays open and lists "By city / metro" disabled with the reason) and Coverage (`core` = the atlas's own ecosystems, `all` = every published country / top-100 cities). Industry (1st/2nd/3rd biggest) re-points F15 at that industry (`inds`), enabled only when `industryRanks`; Flow picks a variant of measures published as several numbers (`variants`: Unicorns stock/new/exits, T094 in/out/net, T114, T115), which also switches unit and direction. Ranks follow the direction; colors use the rank percentile in 5 steps (`Map/Beacon *` variables, darker ranks higher) so one outlier does not wash out the map.
- Level city: beacons per city/metro (count measures as proportional bubbles; over 30 rows beacons shrink and only the top 12 get labels) and host countries tinted. Level country: countries filled by step + small beacons at the country anchor (largest polygon centroid, or a fixed point for countries missing from the 110m map; 36 tiny territories stay rankings-only).
- Profile card: ecosystem select (rows of the current view), meta (country · region), an extra line with the industry (F15/F08) or the full variant set (a net is never shown without its gross), `Value <year>` + Rank n/N, and the last six years as bars scaled to the entity's own min-max.
- Labels avoid each other and the beacons; values use text colors for contrast.
- The round filter button (`[data-atlas-filters-toggle]`) collapses the whole panel (filters + results) into just the button, like closing a popup: a clip-path shrinks the panel into the button, then the freed space closes; expanding reverses it. State: `.atlas_panel.is-collapsed`, `aria-expanded` on the button. The second filter row is always visible.
- Filters are native `<select>`s filled from the JSON (block, measure, coverage, industry, flow, level) and enhanced into custom dropdowns by `src/utils/dropdown.js` (`enhanceSelect`). The `<select>` stays as the source of truth (hidden, `aria-hidden`); the trigger reuses its Webflow class (`atlas_select`, `atlas_profile-select`). Keyboard: Enter/Space/arrows open, arrows/Home/End move, letters type-ahead, Enter picks, Esc closes. Industry and Flow are disabled when not applicable (title explains why); Level is never disabled, only its city option.
- The beacon of the entity shown in the profile (selected, or the leader) gets a white ring.
- Ranking `<ol>` and profile card stay in sync with the map (hover highlights, click selects and centers the globe; clicking the selected row or beacon again keeps it selected, so a double click never jumps back to the leader; rows are not text-selectable). Ranking and year bars animate from their previous width (or 0 on first load) to the new one, staggered 30ms per row (`animateBars`).
- Measure Details band (`[data-atlas-measure]`) takes texts from the Measures CMS card with the same `data-def` key (= measure code, e.g. `F01`; 21 items loaded from the lab texts); falls back to the lab texts in the JSON. Unit, direction and observed range follow the current selection.
- Drawer (`[data-atlas-drawer]`): tabs Data scope / Methodology / Trend figure / Definitions & formulas, prev/next, Esc and overlay close, focus trap, arrow keys on tabs. Motion (WAAPI): overlay fades, panel slides in from the right (from the bottom on mobile) with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, rail buttons pop in staggered, tabs and content rise a beat later; closing reverses faster with an ease-in, then hides. Tab changes slide the content in from the side of the new tab. Panel children never shrink (`flex-shrink: 0`), so a long trend table scrolls instead of crushing the tab bar. Trend figure draws one line per location of the current view (top 8 by rank colored, the rest muted, gaps break the line, negative values supported) + a locations × years table. Definitions cards get block anchors, "Show on map" and "Read more".
- Deep link: `?m=CODE&e=entity&v=flat|globe&b=capital|talent&c=core|all&l=city|country&i=0-2&f=variant&tab=`.

## Updating the data

When the lab sends a new platform export, replace `prototype/ecosystem-platform/index.html` (or pass a JSON file with the same `window.ATLAS` object) and run `npm run atlas:build`. Shape of `atlas.json`: `meta`, `blocks`, `notes[]`, `entities[]` (`id, name, country, region, city, metro, lat, lng, iso`), `measures[]` (`key` = lab code, texts, `unit`, `direction`, `cityLevel`, `industryRanks`, `variants`, `kind`), `values[key] = [[entityIndex, core, noteIndex, [[year, value, n, extra?], ...]], ...]` where `extra` may hold `inds`, `top`, `vals`, `cnts`. A new measure also needs a Measures CMS item whose "Data key" is its code.
