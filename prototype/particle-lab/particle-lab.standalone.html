Our mission

Mapping how ideas become economies

From Silicon Valley to Nairobi and beyond, we study what startups need to scale, how investors find opportunity, and how institutions and networks shape the world’s next entrepreneurial hubs.

Our Lab builds uniquely broad, globally distributed data and combines it with economic experiments and close partnerships with founders, investors, companies, and governments.
